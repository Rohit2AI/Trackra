About TRACKRA

TRACKRA is an intelligent map-based web application designed to enhance road safety and real-time navigation by visualizing critical road conditions directly on live maps. The system focuses on identifying and representing real-world road hazards such as potholes, waterlogging, speed breakers, and traffic congestion using road-aligned visual overlays instead of traditional area-based representations.

Unlike conventional mapping solutions, TRACKRA emphasizes road-level intelligence by integrating geospatial data processing, real-time location tracking, and dynamic visualization techniques. The platform enables users to search locations, access nearby essential services (including hospitals, petrol pumps, and police stations), and view contextual environmental data such as weather conditions associated with specific geographic coordinates.

The primary objective of TRACKRA is to bridge the gap between static digital maps and real-world road conditions by providing an interactive, user-centric, and scalable solution for smarter mobility and safer travel.

This project has been independently conceptualized, designed, and developed as part of an innovative exploration into intelligent map-based systems.
